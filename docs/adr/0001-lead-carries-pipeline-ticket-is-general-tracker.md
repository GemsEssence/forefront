# Lead carries the full sales pipeline; Ticket is a general Customer task tracker

Forefront's `Lead` represents a prospect's entire journey with exactly one `Product`, from first contact through `won`/`lost` — there is no separate `Deal`/`Opportunity`/`Sale` model, and a won Lead is itself the record of sale that Payments/Installments attach to. `Ticket` is a general-purpose action tracker tied to a Customer (optionally a Lead) covering demo scheduling, support issues, complaints, feature requests, and renewal reminders; it carries no revenue data and is not a sales-pipeline or support-only concept. This was chosen over splitting into a separate `Deal` and `SupportRequest`: the existing `Lead` enum already modeled the full pipeline (`proposal`, `negotiation`, `nda_signed`, `won`, `lost`) and `Ticket`'s category taxonomy (`demo`, `plan_expired`, `complaint`, `new_requirement`, ...) already matched the general-tracker shape — splitting further would only recreate the same two concepts under new names. A Lead supports exactly one Product; multi-product line items were considered and explicitly rejected for simplicity.

**Amended (2026-09-29):** "optionally a Lead" is now real: a Ticket can be converted into a Lead (becoming its first Ticket), and a Lead has many Tickets for the sales work done under it. Renewal Tickets remain unattached to any Lead (see ADR 0003).
