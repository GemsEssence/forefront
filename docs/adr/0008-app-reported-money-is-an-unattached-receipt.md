# Money reported by a Product's application is an unattached Receipt, never a Payment, and is never auto-attached

When a Customer pays inside a Product's own application, that application calls `POST /api/v1/receipts` (same per-Product Bearer key as Signup). The reference CRM design calls this "a payment the Sales person attaches". In Forefront's language a Payment is what a Customer *owes* on a won Lead, set by the Sales person when the sale closes, and a Receipt is money that has *arrived*; what the application reports is the latter, so it is stored as a Receipt with no Payment yet (an "unattached Receipt") and carries the Product, the amount, the application's own reference (idempotency key per Product) and the Customer when the phone number matched one.

Staff attach it by hand to exactly one Payment or Installment, which is when the ordinary settlement rules run. We deliberately do not auto-attach even when the Customer has a single won Lead for that Product with money owed: the Payment total and Installment plan are Sales decisions, an unexpected amount usually means one of them is wrong, and a silent match would hide that. The attach form pre-selects the obvious target instead, and a Manager or Admin may discard a duplicate or test Receipt with a note.
